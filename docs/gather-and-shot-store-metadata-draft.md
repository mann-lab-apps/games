## Full Description

Gather & Shot is a one-touch snow survival game built around a simple risk decision.

Move to stay safe. Stop to gather snow. When snowman enemies get close, your character automatically throws snowballs. Every pause can refill your ammo, but every pause also gives the snowmen time to close in.

Defeat enemies and collect Snow Coin to improve your next run in the Snow Workshop. Upgrade ammo capacity, gather speed, throw rate, snowball damage, warm coat protection, and pickup magnet range.

Discover stronger snow attacks as you play:

- Big Snowball clears packed groups.
- Split Snowball breaks into extra shots.
- Ice Shot flies fast and hits hard.
- Snow Burst blasts nearby enemies.
- Rapid Throw speeds up automatic attacks.

Snowman enemy waves change as you survive. Walkers teach the core loop, Runners pressure your movement, and Heavy snowmen test your upgrades.

Every run gives you a clear next goal: gather snow, defeat enemies, collect big snowdrifts, survive new waves, and return to the Workshop stronger.
