## Full Description

Stop & Snow is a stop-to-gather snowfield survival game built around one clear risk decision.

Move to stay safe. Stop to build snow. The longer you stand still, the bigger your snowball becomes: Small, Packed, then Giant. Fresh snow patches and snowdrifts refill your snowball ammo faster, but standing still gives snowmen time to surround you. When enemies enter range, your character automatically throws the snowball you built, so the challenge is choosing where and when to stop.

Your character visibly changes between running, building a snowball, throwing, and taking a hit, making the stop-and-risk loop readable even in a silent preview.

Snow is not just a counter. It is a map resource:

- Small Snow Patches provide quick emergency ammo.
- Big Snowdrifts gather faster and can trigger Big Snowball attacks.
- Icy Snowdrifts can unlock fast Ice Shot pressure clears.
- Repeated gathering visibly shrinks a snow resource until it is depleted.
- Built snowballs change attack results: Small is quick, Packed hits harder, and Giant can clear clustered snowmen.

Defeat snowmen and deplete snowdrifts to earn Snow Coin. After each run, visit the Snow Gear Lab and upgrade your winter kit:

- Snow Pouch: carry more snowballs.
- Wool Gloves: gather snow faster.
- Throw Mitts: auto throw more often.
- Packed Core: increase snowball damage.
- Warm Coat: survive more contact damage.
- Magnet Charm: pull rewards from farther away.

Snowman waves change as you survive. Small Walkers teach the stop-to-gather loop, red-scarf Runners force movement, and large Heavy snowmen test your upgrades.

Every run gives a visible next goal across Frost Yard, Snow Patch Field, Red Scarf Lane, Heavy Snowbank, and Blizzard Gate: mine a snow patch, stop incoming snowmen, gather fresh snow, deplete a big snowdrift, survive the red-scarf wave, and return to the lab stronger.

## Apple 4.3(a) Differentiation Emphasis

- The app should not be described as a generic runner, idle game, battle game, or arcade shooter.
- Lead with the unique stop-to-gather snow resource mechanic.
- Show snow resource depletion in screenshots or preview video.
- Show the player pose change from running to building snowballs to auto throwing.
- Show snowman enemy role differences in screenshots.
- Mention named route zones rather than presenting the app as a single endless score arena.
- Show Snow Gear Lab as the persistent progression screen.
- Avoid metadata that could resemble template casual games with only score, coins, and generic upgrades.
