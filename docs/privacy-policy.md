# Mann Lab Games Privacy Policy

Mann Lab games run locally on the device and use local storage for best scores,
settings, and game progress where needed.

Some released or release-candidate games, including 2048 Crash, 2048 Blink,
Thumbwaddle, Best Ramyeon, Stop & Snow, and 1 = 1 may use Firebase Analytics for
gameplay event analytics, Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics, and,
where production ad IDs are configured, Google AdMob for advertising at natural
breaks such as game over.

Firebase Analytics may collect app usage and device diagnostics to help improve
stability and gameplay balance. Firebase Crashlytics may collect crash logs and
diagnostic data when the app crashes. When ads are enabled for a submitted
build, Google AdMob may collect advertising data, device identifiers, coarse
location, diagnostics, and interaction data for ad delivery, frequency capping,
fraud prevention, analytics, and third-party advertising.

The games do not require account creation, user-generated content, payment
processing, online leaderboards, or multiplayer services unless a specific game
states otherwise in its own release notes.
